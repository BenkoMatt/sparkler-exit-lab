## F1. Why sparklers vanish under flash — VERIFIED
- TTL/Auto flash overpowers the scene and kills the sparkler glow. [Rangefinder: "If you set your flash to Auto or TTL, the flash will overpower the scene and you won't get that romantic sparkler vibe"; SLR Lounge: flash exposed below ambient is invisible, above it dominates]
- Flash duration (~1/1000s+) freezes ember bursts as tiny points; sparkler brightness in image depends on ISO + aperture, NOT shutter speed. [Printique/Adorama: "ISO and APERTURE do affect the brightness of the sparkler... shutter speed does not"]
- Fast shutter + front-curtain = no trails at all.

## F2. The fix recipe — VERIFIED
- Rear-curtain sync: flash fires at the END of the exposure; motion blur recorded early trails BEHIND the sharp subject. [Digital Camera World; Great Big Photography World; HubPages — 3 independent]
- Shutter drag: slow shutter (≈1/15–1/60s typical) + flash to freeze subjects, ambient/sparklers burn in. [SLR Lounge; nichilton.com f/2.8–5.6 dance-floor guidance]
- ISO 800–1600 (Mark Davidson: 800–1000); aperture f/1.4–2.8 for exits; FEC ≈ −2/3 to −1⅔ so flash stays under ambient. [Reddit r/WeddingPhotography threads; Mark Davidson guide]
- AF-assist beam on, AF-s off Eye-AF (focus jumps to guests). [Katch Silva; Andy Saywell]
