Method & Limitations

How the visibility analysis actually works, where it can be wrong, and the published sources behind the guidance on this page.

How the analysis works

Everything runs client-side in JavaScript, entirely in your browser. The analyzer builds a luminance histogram from the image data, performs bright-spot and streak detection to locate sparkler trails, and assembles a 0–100 visibility score from three components: trail length, ember density, and a highlight-clipping penalty.

EXIF metadata — shutter speed, ISO, aperture, and the flash-fired flag — is read locally from the file itself.

Nothing is uploaded. There is no server, no accounts, and no AI or cloud vision is involved anywhere in the pipeline.

Limitations

  • The score is a heuristic estimate, not a professional review of your photograph.
  • Very dark or heavily cropped photos can fool the detector and skew the result.
  • HEIC files are not decoded outside Safari — convert to JPEG first.
  • The demo photos are procedurally synthesized, not real wedding photographs.
  • Color tinting of heads is an artistic overlay, not a physical simulation of light.
